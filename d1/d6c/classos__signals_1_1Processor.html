#include <userver/os_signals/processor.hpp>

Detailed Description

A client that allows you to subscribe to OS signals SIGUSR1 and SIGUSR2.

Usually retrieved from os_signals::ProcessorComponent. For tests use os_signals::ProcessorMock.
