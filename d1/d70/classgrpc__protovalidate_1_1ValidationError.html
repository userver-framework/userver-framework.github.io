enum class grpc_protovalidate::ValidationError::Type
strong

Type of validation error.

Enumerator
kInternal 

Validation failed due to validator internal errors. In most cases this indicates protovalidate CEL expression errors in the proto file.

kRule 

Validation failed due to violations of protovalidate constraints by the input message.
